Pine Labs Online prototype

Tap to pay with UPI in Muse

  1. 1Muse

    Muse checks out in its browser

    Finds the product, fills the cart, address and delivery.

  2. 2Pine Labs Online

    Pine Labs Online generates the UPI payment link

    One link for this exact order and amount.

  3. 3UPI app

    Customer taps and pays in their UPI app

    They confirm with their own UPI PIN.

  4. 4Muse

    Back in Muse, order complete

    Muse confirms the payment and tracks the order.

Clickable prototype. All data is mocked.
